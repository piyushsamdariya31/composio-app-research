1The patterns

The headline first. Every number is computed from the dataset, so the claims and the table cannot drift apart.

  1. Most of the list is buildable today. 71 of 100 apps can become a toolkit now with credentials a developer gets alone; 22 need a review, a paid plan or a key request first; only 7 need a deal or have no public API.
  2. Auth is a two-lane road, not an OAuth monopoly. OAuth2 is the right primary for 50 apps and a static key or token for 45. 45 apps offer both, so a toolkit framework has to treat API keys as first-class, not as a fallback.
  3. Gating follows the category, not the company size. Developer, Infra and Data platforms, Productivity and Project Management are 10/10 self-serve. Ads platforms gate on app review, fintech on account approval or partnership, AI-native apps on paid plans, ecommerce on a mix: Marketing, Ads, Email and Social 5/10; Ecommerce 5/10; AI, Research and Media-native 5/10; Finance and Fintech 5/10.
  4. The most common blocker is a tie: app review (11) and paid plans (11). Partnership gates are rarer (6) and cluster in enterprise and fintech apps.
  5. Easy wins vs outreach. Of the 23 apps without a full Composio toolkit, 9 are ready to build now (LiveAgent, Vonage, systeme.io, WooCommerce, Magento (Adobe Commerce), Netlify, Binance, iPayX, Reducto); 11 need outreach or a sponsored account.
  6. MCP is now table stakes, but "official MCP" can mean docs search. 60 apps have a vendor MCP that can act on account data. Twilio, Mailchimp and Vonage ship MCP servers that only search their docs, and Slack’s MCP is open only to Marketplace-listed or internal apps.

Moving targets: facts that changed in 2025-26, which a model's memory gets wrong

  • Shopify Admin-created custom apps (static tokens) can no longer be created; Storefront MCP was removed in favour of Shop MCP / UCP.
  • Salesforce Connected-app creation restricted from Spring '26; hosted MCP servers went GA in April 2026 for Enterprise Edition and up.
  • Zendesk API tokens are marked deprecated; OAuth is the supported method.
  • Xero 2026 developer pricing: free Starter tier capped at 5 connections, paid tiers above.
  • fanbasis Rebranded to Commas; APIs offered to enterprises through sales.
  • Amazon SP-API Docs moved to developer-docs.amazon.

This is why research has to be re-run against live docs, and why the first pass, which answered from memory, missed most on MCP (6 of its 20 sampled MCP answers wrong) and on access (3 wrong).
